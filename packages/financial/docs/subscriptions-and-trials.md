# Subscriptions, trials, renewals, and add-ons

Create a recurring price (`day`, `week`, `month`, or `year`, with positive `intervalCount`) and map it to entitlement catalog keys. A trusted agent calls `billing.startSubscription({ actor, audit, userId, priceId, provider?, providerReference? })`. The service rejects one-off or inactive prices. If `trialDays>0` and the account has never had a trial for this product, it creates a `trialing` subscription and bounded entitlement grants ending at `trialEndsAt`. Trial reuse creates an `incomplete` subscription without free access. This check and grant run in a serializable transaction. The host must decide whether a trial can start without a payment method and how to notify before it ends.

For the first paid period, issue an invoice with `subscriptionId` and a service period on its line. The service requires the invoice price to match the subscription. Bind it to a provider and settle it as in [invoices](./invoices.md). On full settlement, the service inserts a unique `subscription_renewals` row for the invoice and period start, marks the subscription active, advances its current period, grants the price's entitlements through the service end, and emits `financial.subscription.renewed`. A failed or partial payment does not extend access.

The host schedules `billing.issueRenewalInvoice({ actor, audit, userId, subscriptionId, taxRateBps, couponCode?, dueAt? })` when `currentPeriodEndsAt` or `trialEndsAt` is due. It calculates the next UTC calendar period using the immutable price frequency and count, with month-end clamping (October 31 to November 30). The invoice key is derived from subscription ID and period start, so repeating the job returns the same invoice. A future period, inactive price, canceled subscription, or scheduled cancellation fails. The caller supplies a fresh jurisdiction-aware tax rate snapshot each period. The service does not charge a card automatically; the host must create/collect a provider payment and reconcile its signed result.

`billing.cancelSubscription` schedules cancellation at the current period end and emits `financial.subscription.cancellation_scheduled`. It does not revoke a paid grant early. The host calls `billing.finishSubscription` after the end, which marks it canceled and emits `financial.subscription.canceled_local`; grants already have their own end dates. If a provider subscription exists, update/cancel the provider first and reconcile local state. Proration, plan switching, and automatic provider subscription creation are not implemented; use separate credited invoice lines and an explicit migration flow rather than mutating a historical invoice.

`ProductAddOn` links a base product to an independently priced add-on. The catalog relation itself grants nothing. Sell the add-on with a separate price/invoice or subscription so its own entitlement mappings and renewal period are auditable.
